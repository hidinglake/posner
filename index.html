How to play

Two symbols appear. Press the most specific thing they share before the white bar runs out. Every pair has one right answer: MEANING and SYSTEM beat DOMAIN, and DOMAIN beats UNRELATED.

01 Meaning

The same thing, written two different ways.

PairWhy
VIII · 8Both mean 8: Roman and Arabic
Fe · IronBoth mean iron: symbol and name
β · BetaBoth mean beta: letter and name
02 System

Written the same way, but two different things.

PairWhy
III · XIIBoth Roman: 3 and 12
He · LiBoth symbols: helium and lithium
λ · πBoth letters: lambda and pi
03 Domain

The same kind of thing, but a different meaning and a different way of writing it.

PairWhy
IV · 12Both numbers: 4 in Roman, 12 in Arabic
Na · OxygenBoth elements: sodium as a symbol, oxygen as a name
δ · SigmaBoth Greek: delta as a letter, sigma as a name
04 Unrelated

Nothing in common: one item from each of two different sets. Only appears when two or more sets are ticked.

PairWhy
7 · HeA number and an element
Iron · πAn element and a Greek letter
Theta · XIIA Greek letter and a number

PairAnswerNot

Content

Each set has exactly two ways of writing its items. Tick one set or mix several.

SetWays of writingExample
NumbersArabic, Roman12 · XII
ElementsSymbol, nameFe · Iron
GreekLetter, nameβ · Beta

When sets are mixed, anything that could be read as an item from another set is left out, so no pair has two answers. For example, ν (nu) reads as V, so it doesn't appear alongside Roman numerals or element symbols.
